About

I am a Robotics & AI Research Intern at KITECH, with a background in Mechatronics Engineering and Intelligent Robotics. My hands-on work has focused on ROS 2-based mobile robots, robotic manipulation, and integrating sensing, planning, and control into complete robot systems.

Building on that systems experience, I am strengthening my deep learning foundations and exploring robot learning and multimodal approaches that connect perception and language to robotic action.
