I am currently strengthening my Deep Learning foundations while studying VLM, VLA, and Robot Learning, with a focus on connecting perception and language understanding to robot action.

Research Interests

  • Vision-Language Models — visual information과 language understanding의 결합
  • Vision-Language-Action Models — multimodal understanding을 robot action으로 연결하는 방법
  • Robot Learning — demonstration과 interaction을 통해 skill과 policy를 학습하는 방법
  • Robotic Manipulation — perception, planning, control을 연결한 object manipulation
  • Embodied AI — physical environment에서 perception, reasoning, action을 통합하는 agent

I am particularly interested in connecting visual-language understanding with high-level planning and robotic manipulation.
